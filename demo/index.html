The Tool Shed

An interactive demo. The website and the phone app below share one set of sample data, so what you do in one shows up in the other.

Try this

  1. On the phone, sign in as Ben, open the Cordless drill and send a request.
  2. On the website you are Olive, the drill's owner. Open Loans, then approve Ben's request with a note.
  3. Back on the phone, Loans shows Approved with Olive's note, and the drill's calendar is red on both screens.
  4. On the website, switch to Priya and ask for the same dates. They are already spoken for.
  5. Invite someone. On the website as Olive, open Invitations and create a link. On the phone, sign out, choose "I have an invitation" and paste it.
  6. Photos. On My tools, list a tool and press "Try an SVG disguised as a photo". It is refused, because the file's contents decide, not its name.
